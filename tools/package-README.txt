THE NIGHTMARE BEFORE CHRISTMAS: OOGIE'S REVENGE - a PC port of the original Xbox game, by JosiahG

Needs your own copy of the game: Oogie's Revenge for the original Xbox, USA (title 42560004).
A disc image (Redump .iso or xiso) or a folder extracted from it. No game data is included.

1. Open "Oogie's Revenge.exe".
2. The first time, choose "Choose your disc..." and pick your disc image (or the default.xbe in an extracted game
   folder). Its files are copied into data\Disc once. After that it opens straight to the game's own menu, drawn
   with the game's art from your disc.

The game's menu (arrow keys, Enter and Esc, or a gamepad):
  Play: start the game.
  Settings: resolution (up to 4K), picture shape (4:3 up to ultrawide 21:9 and 32:9: wider shows more of the
    world, the HUD and movies keep 4:3), frame rate (60, or 120 with smooth in-between frames; matches your screen
    by default), ray tracing (ray-traced ambient occlusion: soft shadows in corners and under characters), window
    or fullscreen, volume and music. Saved at once.
  Controls: every keyboard key can be changed (left/right switches to the gamepad list), and every gamepad button:
    choose one, then press the new button. Xbox-style gamepads work right away; the sticks stay as they are.
  Mods: turn the mods in data\UserData\mods on or off.
During play, F1 opens the same menu over the paused game (F1, Esc or B goes back). Sound, keys, ray tracing,
window and the frame rate in the title change at once; resolution, picture shape and frame rate the next time the game
starts.
To set up a different disc, run "Oogie's Revenge.exe" --setup.
Updating from an older release: unzip over it. Your disc files, saves and settings in data\ are kept.
Keyboard (default): WASD move, arrow keys camera, Space A, Left Shift B, R X, F Y, Q/E triggers,
1/2 Black/White, Enter Start, Tab Back, IJKL D-pad.
In game: F11 or Alt+Enter = fullscreen / window.

Saves, settings and mods are in data\UserData. To update, unzip the new release over this folder.
If something goes wrong, data\UserData\log.txt says what happened.
